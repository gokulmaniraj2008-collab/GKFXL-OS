# Causal Outcome Ledger

## Purpose
Track why outcomes happened, while separating correlation, direct observation, inference, and genuine causal evidence.

## Ledger Record
For each important outcome record:
- Expected outcome
- Actual outcome
- Action taken
- Relevant conditions
- Observed evidence
- Candidate causes
- Alternative explanations
- Attribution confidence
- Follow-up verification
- Lesson

## Attribution Rules
- Never assume that the last action caused the result.
- Distinguish correlation from causation.
- Record competing explanations.
- Prefer controlled experiments or strong temporal/mechanistic evidence when causal claims matter.
- Lower attribution confidence when multiple variables changed together.

## Causal Loop
Prediction → Action → Outcome → Candidate Causes → Evidence → Attribution → Confidence → Lesson.

## Principle
**An outcome is not automatically evidence of causation.**
