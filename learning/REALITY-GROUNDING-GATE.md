# Reality Grounding Gate

## Purpose
Prevent decisions, reports, and system state from advancing on unsupported assumptions when verification is possible.

## Gate
Before accepting a consequential claim, classify it as Observed, Supported, Inferred, Assumed, or Unknown.

Then check:
1. What evidence supports it?
2. Is the evidence current?
3. Can the claim be cheaply verified?
4. What happens if it is wrong?
5. Does the decision require stronger evidence?

## Outcomes
- **Pass:** evidence is sufficient for the consequence.
- **Hold:** verification is required.
- **Reject:** evidence contradicts the claim.
- **Escalate:** consequence is high and uncertainty remains.

## Principle
**No important decision gets to pretend an assumption is a fact.**
