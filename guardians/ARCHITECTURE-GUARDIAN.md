# Architecture Guardian

## Purpose
Keep the system understandable, maintainable, reliable, and proportionate to the actual problem.

## Architecture Checks
- Prefer the simplest architecture that satisfies the requirement.
- Separate responsibilities at meaningful boundaries.
- Avoid premature abstraction and unnecessary services.
- Protect critical data and control flows with explicit contracts.
- Review dependencies, failure modes, scalability, observability, and operational cost.
- Preserve reversibility for uncertain decisions.

## Anti-Overengineering Rule
Complexity must earn its place through a measurable requirement, risk reduction, or validated need.

## Decision Record
For significant architecture choices record: problem → options → decision → trade-offs → evidence.
