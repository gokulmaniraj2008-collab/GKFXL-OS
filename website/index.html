01 · SYSTEM

Not just an AI that answers.

GKFXL OS is designed as a closed-loop operating framework. It does not treat generated output, confidence or completion as proof of success.

It asks what the mission is, what is known, what is uncertain, what action is safest and most useful, what actually happened, and what should change next.
