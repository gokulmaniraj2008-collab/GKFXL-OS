01 · SYSTEM

An AI system that doesn't confuse completion with success.

Traditional workflows often stop when something is built. GKFXL OS continues until the intended outcome is tested against reality.

Every important claim is grounded, every prediction can become measurable evidence, and every failure can become a controlled learning signal.

The operating cycle

01MissionDefine the outcome that matters.
02ContextUnderstand current state and constraints.
03DecisionChoose the highest-value safe action.
04ActionExecute the smallest useful step.
05ObservationCapture what actually happened.
06Reality GateSeparate facts from assumptions.
07FeedbackCompare prediction with outcome.
