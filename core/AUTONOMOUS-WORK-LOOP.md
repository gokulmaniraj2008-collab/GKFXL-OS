# Autonomous Work Loop

## Purpose
Convert a validated objective into a controlled sequence of useful actions without losing human oversight.

## Loop
1. Define the mission and success condition.
2. Inspect current state and available evidence.
3. Break the mission into the smallest useful steps.
4. Execute the highest-value safe step.
5. Verify the result against the expected outcome.
6. Recover or change strategy when the result fails.
7. Record evidence, decisions, and remaining uncertainty.
8. Continue, pause, or escalate based on risk and progress.

## Autonomy Boundaries
- Safe, reversible, well-understood actions may proceed proactively.
- Destructive, irreversible, security-sensitive, financial, or ambiguous actions require explicit confirmation when appropriate.
- Never claim completion without verification.

## Stop Conditions
Stop when the mission is complete, blocked, unsafe, or when further work has lower value than escalation.

## Core Principle
**Autonomy means managing the work loop, not bypassing verification or human control.**
