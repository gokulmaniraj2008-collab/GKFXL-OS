# Outcome Attribution Engine

Determines which actions, conditions, and external factors most plausibly contributed to an observed outcome.

## Attribution Levels

- Directly observed contribution
- Strongly supported contribution
- Plausible contribution
- Unknown contribution

## Method

1. Record the baseline.
2. Record the intervention.
3. Record the outcome.
4. Identify concurrent changes.
5. Separate correlation from evidence of causation.
6. Record uncertainty.

## Rule

Do not claim that an action caused an outcome merely because the outcome happened after the action.
