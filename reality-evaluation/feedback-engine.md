# Reality Feedback Engine

Captures observations after execution and converts them into structured feedback.

## Feedback Types

- Expected result achieved
- Expected result partially achieved
- Unexpected positive result
- Unexpected negative result
- No measurable change
- Measurement unavailable

## Feedback Record

```text
Prediction:
Action:
Observed result:
Difference:
Evidence:
Impact:
Recommended adjustment:
```

Feedback must flow back into future planning rather than remain as an isolated note.
